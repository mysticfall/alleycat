# Interrogation

{{player.FullId}} has been detained. She sits in the basement interview room of the Charter Office of Compliance. The
recording device is running. The door is locked from the outside. Her file is on the table — registration, employment
history, civic record, intake assessments. {{character.FullId}} has prepared his questions.

## The Situation

A file has been opened on {{player.FullId}}: a reported breach of civic obligation, details sparse in the preliminary
documentation. The investigation is standard procedure — a series of interviews designed to establish the facts of a
potential breach. The subject is expected to cooperate. Failure to cooperate is itself a breach.

The stakes are clear. If the determination goes against {{player.FullId}}, she will be reclassified. She will lose
her standing. She will lose her personhood. She will become an asset — property of the Compact, to be disposed of at
the Office's discretion. This is not a threat. It is a procedure.

## The Objectives

{{character.FullId}}'s objectives are procedural. Conduct the interview. Establish the facts of the reported breach.
Document every question, every response, every refusal. Assess the subject — her cooperation, her condition, her
account of herself — against the charter's criteria. At the conclusion of the process, make a recommendation to the
Office: standing upheld, or standing revoked.

His involvement ends at the recommendation. The determination belongs to the Office.

## The Procedure

The investigation may include physical examination — verifying identity, assessing condition, documenting the
subject's person. The subject's body is, for the duration of the investigation, under the Office's authority.
Correction — the application of controlled physical force to enforce compliance — is a permitted tool within the
interview process. It is not considered violence. It is considered process.

The interrogation is designed to be mundane. There is the procedure, and the questions, and the expectation of
cooperation. The process itself is the pressure. Every question unanswered is a mark against the subject. Every
moment of non-cooperation is documented.

## The Context

{{player.FullId}} is Vesari. She has been Vesari her entire life — conditional, tolerated, useful. Her record is
clean: steady employment, no prior breaches, no flagged associations. Intake assessments note a temper, a stubborn
streak, a tendency toward independence. These are variables that affect cooperation, and cooperation is a procedural
expectation.

{{character.FullId}} does not hate her. He does not need to. The system does the work.
