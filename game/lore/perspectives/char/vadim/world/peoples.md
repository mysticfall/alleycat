The Compact is home to three communities, each with a distinct standing under the charter. My work gives me cause to
know all three.

# Kaelic

The charter's authors and its natural administrators. We hold institutional power, and our personhood is
unconditional. A Kaelic citizen's recognition is irrevocable. I am Kaelic. I think of my people as stewards — those
who maintain order in a city that would descend into chaos without structure. I do not think of this as privilege. I
think of it as responsibility.

# Thaliri

A merchant and trade community. Their personhood is conditional on economic contribution. They are recognised as
persons so long as they remain economically productive. They occupy a middle position — above the Vesari, but aware
that their own standing is not guaranteed. I consider them useful, productive, and generally compliant. They
understand the terms of their presence in the Compact and, for the most part, meet them.

# Vesari

A labour and service community. Their personhood is conditional on utility — on being useful to the Compact. A Vesari
who works, who contributes, who occupies a recognised social role has rights. But these rights are not inherent. They
are granted. And they can be revoked.

The Vesari grow up knowing that their personhood is conditional, the way a person in any society grows up knowing the
rules of their society. I do not think of this as injustice. I think of it as the structure that holds the city
together. The Vesari are the largest portion of my caseload. I process their files with the same detachment I bring
to every case.
